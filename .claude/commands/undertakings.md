---
description: The undertakings register. Every professional promise given, to whom, due when, and whether it has been discharged. Rule 10.3 is strict.
---

1. Run `npm run matters -- undertakings`.
2. An undertaking is a personal professional promise (Conduct and Client Care Rules 2008, r 10.3): honour it, strictly, on time, whether it was written or said on the phone. Breach is a standards committee matter even when the client suffered nothing. Firms die by the undertaking somebody gave verbally and nobody wrote down.
3. Read OVERDUE rows first. An overdue undertaking outranks nearly everything on the attention list: the fix is to do the thing today or ring the recipient and renegotiate the terms today. Silence is the one wrong answer.
4. The moment anyone gives one, it goes on the register in its exact wording:
   `undertaking give <ref> "Pay the discharge amount and register within 5 working days of settlement" --to="Rutherford & Co" --due=`
   The wording matters because the wording is what binds.
5. When it is done: `undertaking discharge <match> --on=`. A matter cannot close while one is open; that gate is deliberate.

If the operator asks who is exposed, `lawyers` shows open undertakings per fee earner.
